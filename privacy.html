What we collect

If you email us, including through the pilot request button, we receive what you choose to send: usually your name, company, work email address, role and, optionally, a description of your current tooling. The button only opens a draft in your own email app. Nothing reaches us until you send it.

Please don't include confidential information or personal data about other people.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use your enquiry to reply to you and to discuss a possible pilot. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: responding to a business enquiry you have chosen to make. If we go on to agree a pilot, we will use the details needed to set up and manage that agreement.

We will not add you to a marketing list unless you ask us to.

Who can access it

Your email is processed on our behalf by our email service provider. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like most web services, they may process technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests the page. These providers may process data outside the UK under their own safeguards. This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless we enter into an agreement with you, in which case we keep records for as long as that agreement and the law require.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email hello@swiftfortress.live and we will respond within one month.
